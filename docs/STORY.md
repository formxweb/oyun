# VERTIGO: ABOVE THE FALL — Story Bible

> *Every fall reveals something.*

## One sentence

VERTIGO is a vertical parkour adventure where falling reveals the world.

## The question

**Why was the city built upward?**

## The answer (revealed only at the top)

It wasn't. The city was built **downward**.

Thirty generations ago a small people lived on the summit of the Pillar — a stone spire
that rises out of the clouds. They knew nothing but the cloud sea below them. Their oldest
song says *"we fell up"*: they do not know how they came to be above.

After a great storm tore a hole in the clouds, a child saw green far, far below. The first
generation decided: *we will reach it.* The way down was two and a half kilometres of sheer
stone, and a fall meant death. So they built. Each generation lowered itself one stratum,
building the next level beneath the last, hanging it from cables and anchoring it to the
Pillar. The great winch — the Machine — lowered stone, timber and people.

Every generation wrote a **Letter Down** to the generation below, and carved **Lessons**
teaching their children how to move without falling: how to run along a wall, how to catch
a ledge, how to roll when you land. Beneath every level they strung the **Fall Lines**: fine
nets, almost invisible, that only show themselves to someone falling fast — so that a child
who slipped would be caught.

The thirtieth generation reached the ground. They called the lowest platform *the Last
Floor*, stepped onto grass, and within two lifetimes forgot the city entirely. The village of
**Lowmark** grew at its foot. Nobody climbs.

## The climber

The player is the first of the ground-born to climb. They carry a **plumb** — a stone bob on a
line — inherited from the grandparent who raised them. It has thirty notches; twenty-nine are
cut. The grandparent was the last child born in the city, carried down in the final descent,
and the only person who ever went back up.

## How the player learns it (without being told)

The climber reads the city **backwards in time**. Every discovery is reinterpreted by the end:

| Seen early | Understood at the top |
|---|---|
| Tally marks carved everywhere (30, 29, 28 …) that **decrease** as you climb | They count generations of the descent. The top is generation 1 — the oldest. |
| The builders' sign: a plumb bob pointing **down** | Not a warning about falling. It means *home is below / onward*. |
| Letters that speak of "making it", then of "waiting", then of "planning" | You are reading the Letters Down in reverse order. |
| Ghostly nets that appear only while falling | The builders' safety nets for their children. The city was built to catch fallers. |
| Carved Lessons that teach movement | Survival training for children of a descending people. |
| The Machine's winch | It lowered the city; it never raised it. |
| Scaffolding hanging *from above* (only visible while falling) | Each level was built beneath the one above it. |
| Storm lighthouses whose lamps point **down** | They guided the way down through the storm. |
| "Above" — the goal | The smallest, oldest place: where it began. |

## Vertical Memory

The world remembers the climber. Changes are authored and causal:
the climber's weight tips a crane, a fall tears an awning, a lightning strike breaks a tower,
restarting the Machine wakes every lift below, a fall line caught once stays solid forever.

## The ending

At the Cradle the climber finds the first letter, and one more: from their grandparent, who
climbed back up once, long ago, and left it for "whoever climbs".

Then the climber steps off.

**The Last Fall** carries them through the entire city in one continuous descent. Every
level's Fall Lines light up beneath them, one after another, and they understand: the city
is a fall that took nine hundred years. They are the first to make it all at once.

They land in the orchard at Lowmark. The final notch on the plumb is cut.

### Complete Journey (all 30 letters)

The grandparent's letter continues: the climber is asked to write the thirty-first letter —
the first **Letter Up**. The epilogue unlocks New Game+: *The Remembered City*.
